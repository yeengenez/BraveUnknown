# Portrait art is Legacy (reference only)

The part images in `parts/` were cut from Canva-generated source images. By the user's decision of
2026-11-18, all Canva-made art is **Legacy: reference only**. These portraits **stay in the game until
replacement art exists**, so heroes still have faces.

When new portrait art arrives, replace the images in `parts/` and keep:
- the `HeroAppearance` contract (body type, class outfit, skin, face, iris, scar, hairstyle, hair colour);
- the ramp-tinting system (`palettes.json`, `ramp_map.gdshader`) and the layer naming in `library.json`.

Full list of Legacy assets: `art_source/LEGACY.md` on the `art-source` branch.
