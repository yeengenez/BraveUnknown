Rules in this test

Turn order. Every unit on both sides acts once per round, highest Attack Speed first. Ties go to higher Luck, then a coin flip. Ten rounds without a winner means the expedition retreats.

Attack Speed. Spd − max(0, weapon Wt + armor Wt − Con), never below 0.

Doubling. If the attacker's AS is 5 or more above the target's, it strikes twice in the same action. No counters.

Hit. Weapon Hit + Skl×2 + Lck/2 − (target AS×2 + Lck). Rolled with two averaged numbers (true hit).

Damage. Str or Mag + Might − Def or Res (armor and shell included). Crit = weapon Crit + Skl/2 − target Lck, triples damage.

Effective. A weapon effective against the target's type doubles its Might.

Melee. Acts from Front or Mid line, and can only target enemies not covered by blocking. Back-line melee waits.

Ranged. Picks the best target instead of rolling. First choice is an enemy it can down this action (most likely hit wins); otherwise the enemy taking the most expected damage (damage × true hit × strikes). Ties go to the lowest HP. If the best target is covered, it moves to the next best uncovered one. Sniper ignores cover.

Flying. Ignores blocking. Rolls target line Front 70 / Mid 20 / Back 10, renormalised over occupied lines. Dive always picks the Back line.

Durability. Weapons lose 1 per strike. Armor loses 1 per hit taken, 2 per crit, and gives no Def or weight once broken. A broken weapon swaps to the next carried one, or to bare hands.

Healer. Heals the ally with the lowest HP % if anyone is at or below 70%. Heal = Mag + staff Power. Each heal uses 1 staff charge.

Injuries. A downed expeditioner is Seriously injured if the final blow was a crit or dealt half their max HP or more; otherwise Lightly injured.
