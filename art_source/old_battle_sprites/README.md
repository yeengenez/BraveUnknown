# Old battle sprites (reference only)

- `user_reference_art.png`: the Soldier, Archer and Cleric reference art provided by the user.
- `base_*.png`: those three characters cut out and reduced to native pixel size (about 120 px tall).
- `anim_ref.py` and `animations/`: attack, hit, skill and critical animations made by moving parts of
  the reference art ("Expedition battle sprites v2"). GIF previews plus transparent sheets, 210×140 per frame.
- `code_drawn_v1/`: the first sprites, drawn entirely in code. Rejected in favour of the reference art.

The new layered battle sprites (`../battle_sprites/`) replace these.
