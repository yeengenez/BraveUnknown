## Locked Archer rules
Quiver and bolt-case rules below **confirmed by the user on 2026-11-19** (after discussing them with ChatGPT).
Still open: how the container shows when the Archer carries a normal bundle (50) and a special bundle (10)
together, and whether "partial" should start after the first shot from a full 50.

- Allowed melee: light simple weapons only — dagger, punching dagger, spiked gauntlet, light mace, sickle.
- Allowed ranged: bow and crossbow.
- Not allowed: spear, shortspear, reach weapons, or two-handed melee weapons.
- The one-handed weapon is held in the right hand.
- Bow release has no arrow on the string. The arrow projectile is a separate combat effect.
- Quiver visibility follows equipped arrows, not the currently held weapon.
- Bolt-case visibility follows equipped bolts, not the currently held weapon.
- If the corresponding ammunition item remains equipped but reaches zero, show the empty container.
- Switching to a dagger must not hide an equipped quiver or bolt case.
- Portrait and battle presentation use the same HeroAppearance and the same back-equipment state.

## Ammunition container states
- `quiver_full`, `quiver_partial`, `quiver_empty`
- `boltcase_full`, `boltcase_partial`, `boltcase_empty`

Normal ammunition bundle: 50. Special ammunition bundle: 10.
